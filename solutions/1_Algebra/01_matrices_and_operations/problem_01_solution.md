
# Problem 01: Matrix Dimensions and Entries

## Question
### Given
Given the matrices:

$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 5
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
B =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
-2 & 3 \\
4 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Find:

1. The dimensions of matrices A and B.
2. The entries A(1,2), A(2,3), B(2,1), and B(3,2).
3. The second row of matrix A and the first column of matrix B as vectors.

## Solution

### 1. Matrix Dimensions

Matrix A has 2 rows and 3 columns.

Therefore, its dimension is 2 × 3.

Matrix B has 3 rows and 2 columns.

Therefore, its dimension is 3 × 2.

### 2. Matrix Entries

Using the row and column indices:

- A(1,2) = -1
- A(2,3) = 5
- B(2,1) = -2
- B(3,2) = 1

### 3. Row and Column Vectors

The second row of matrix A is:

(0, 4, 5)

The first column of matrix B is:

(1, -2, 4)ᵀ

### Explanation and Verification

Matrix dimensions are determined by counting rows and columns.

Each requested entry is identified using its row and column position.

All results were checked against the given matrices.

### Final Results

- A: 2 × 3
- B: 3 × 2
- A(1,2) = -1
- A(2,3) = 5
- B(2,1) = -2
- B(3,2) = 1
